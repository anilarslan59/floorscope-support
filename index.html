Subscriptions

FloorScope Pro subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription from your iPhone's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

If you previously purchased FloorScope Pro and your subscription is not recognized, open FloorScope Settings and select Restore Purchases.
