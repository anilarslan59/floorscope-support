FloorScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

FloorScope is designed as a local-first application. Estimates, customer information, business profile information and related project data are stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

FloorScope does not require you to create an account.

The app may process information you choose to enter, such as customer names, project addresses, business details, measurements, pricing and quote data. This information is used to provide the app's estimating and PDF quote features.

Purchases and Subscriptions

FloorScope Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. FloorScope uses RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlement status. FloorScope does not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Storage

Estimate and business profile data are stored locally on your device. FloorScope does not operate its own cloud account system or backend for storing your estimates.

Third-Party Services

FloorScope uses Apple App Store services for purchases and RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management. These services may process technical information required to provide purchase and subscription functionality.

Children

FloorScope is a professional productivity application and is not directed toward children.
